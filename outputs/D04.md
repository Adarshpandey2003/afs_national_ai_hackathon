## Recommendation

### Raman spectroscopy (In-situ Raman fiber probe with matrix-specific chemometric calibration.): primary

- Raman spectroscopy can provide a molecular fingerprint for direct detection of specific metabolites like glucose, acetate, or lactate and is particularly well-suited for aqueous environments because of low water absorbance compared with infrared methods. *(sourced)* [S01 p.16]

- Glucose and lactate are listed among accessible Raman/NIR variables for bioprocess monitoring. *(sourced)* [S01 p.17]

- Raman intensity depends linearly on concentration over a wide range, so chemometric calibration or classification models can be built. *(sourced)* [S01 p.12]

Applies if: Concentration range and required accuracy must be confirmed with sample spectra.; Fluorescence background and weak Raman signal must be checked; time-gated Raman may be considered if needed.; A suitable bioreactor probe port and sterilisation/CIP compatibility must be confirmed.

### NIR spectroscopy (NIR transmission/transflection probe for clear liquids; diffuse reflection if scattering from cells is high.): comparison

- NIR (780–2500 nm) is the most common online analysis method using fiber optic probes and can be used with transmission or transflection probes for liquids. *(sourced)* [S01 p.10; S01 Table 1 (p.6)]

- NIR is effective for major components like glucose in the millimolar range but may lack sensitivity below 0.1 percent. *(sourced)* [S01 p.16]

Applies if: Cells/turbidity may require transflection or diffuse reflection rather than simple transmission.; Calibration must be matrix-specific.; Visible-NIR silica fiber covers only part of the nominal NIR range; confirm the usable range of the selected system.

### MIR-ATR spectroscopy (MIR-ATR immersion probe.): comparison

- ATR fiber probes enable analysis of liquids in-line or in-situ without sampling, and glucose and lactate are listed as accessible MIR variables. *(sourced)* [S01 p.10; S01 p.17]

- MIR-ATR has high selectivity but average sensitivity and high cost in the comparison table. *(sourced)* [S01 Table 1 (p.6)]

Applies if: Water absorbance is high in MIR, so aeration, path length and ATR contact must be tested.; Fouling and contact at the probe crystal are key constraints.; MIR fiber transmission is partial for common chalcogenide fibers; confirm usable range.

### Fluorescence (Fluorimetric probe as a direct glucose/lactate sensor.): not recommended

- Fluorimetry has high sensitivity but low selectivity and is not a direct measurement of every analyte. *(sourced)* [S01 Table 1 (p.6)]

- Fluorescence is better suited to high-sensitivity detection of cofactors and amino acids like NADH, tryptophan, and riboflavin. *(sourced)* [S01 p.16]

Applies if: Only consider fluorescence as a metabolic-state channel, not as a direct glucose/lactate method, unless a specific fluorescent indicator is justified.
