## Feasibility plan

**Objective:** Turn the undefined biomass and metabolic signals into reference-measurable targets and identify the simplest optical method or combination that tracks them across the fermentation run.

**Samples**

- Representative fermentation broth from early, mid and late/stationary phases of the run.

- Normal batches plus, if possible, batches or perturbations representing the changes the customer wants to detect earlier.

**Reference measurements**

- Off-line dry cell weight or optical density for biomass.

- Viable count or cell count where biomass must be interpreted as viable cells.

- HPLC or enzymatic assay for a named metabolite such as glucose, acetate or lactate, or a validated metabolic indicator.

- Timed supernatant or whole-broth samples matched to each spectral acquisition.

1. **Define target signals and reference assays**: Agree operational definitions: which reference-measurable quantity represents biomass and which analyte or indicator represents the metabolic signal; set the concentration range and acceptance direction. [S01 p.5]

2. **Laboratory spectral screening**: Record candidate methods on the same representative samples with timed reference values; compare usable ranges and high-OD behaviour. [S01 p.16; S01 Table 1 (p.6)]

3. **Build single-method chemometric models**: Preprocess and calibrate each method separately against the independent reference assays; validate on held-out samples or runs before considering data fusion. [S01 p.5]

4. **Test NIR + fluorescence fusion as a hypothesis**: Where single methods are insufficient, test combined NIR and fluorescence against the best single method using aligned sampling and data-fusion chemometrics. [S01 p.7; S01 p.9]

5. **In-line trial and simplification outlook**: Move the promising method to the real measurement point; check fouling, bubbles, temperature and OD changes; if it works, identify key wavelengths for a simpler dedicated optical multisensor system. [S01 p.10; S02 p.3; S05 p.1]

**Comparison:** Compare prediction error, bias and ability to follow changes for UV/Vis turbidity, NIR, fluorescence, and NIR+fluorescence; choose the simplest configuration that meets the client-agreed acceptance targets.

**Go / no-go:** Agree numerical acceptance criteria before testing; go/no-go is based on independent validation samples or runs, not on calibration fit.
