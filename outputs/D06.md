## Feasibility plan

**Objective:** To establish whether water-related spectral features are separable from the petroleum background and to select the most promising method(s) and spectral range(s) before equipment selection; this is method screening, not method validation.

**Samples**

- Representative petroleum stream samples covering the client-agreed water concentration range and water states (dissolved, emulsified, free or vapour if present).

- Samples spanning expected process variation (temperature, pressure, flow, matrix changes, droplet/particle variation).

- A few independent production or archived samples reserved for validation of the screening model.

**Reference measurements**

- The customer's current laboratory water-determination method or standard as the independent reference for every sample.

- Timed/paired reference samples when moving from lab screening to an at-line/in-line trial.

- Documented laboratory precision, uncertainty, sampling time and turnaround to set comparison expectations.

1. **Laboratory screening with candidate methods**: Screen the representative petroleum samples with a multi-method fiber system, starting with MIR-ATR and including NIR and Raman as comparisons; inspect whether water features can be distinguished from the hydrocarbon background across the expected concentration range. [S02 p.3; S02 p.13]

2. **Reference-linked chemometric model**: Build and internally validate matrix-specific calibration or classification models for each candidate method against independent laboratory water results; use single methods first and only test data fusion or a combined probe if one method is likely insufficient. [S01 p.5; S01 p.7; S01 p.10]

3. **At-line/in-line trial at the proposed point**: Test the most promising method at the proposed sample point against timed reference samples; check robustness to real process physical variation and whether the remote fiber link provides the required spectral range and signal quality over the required distance. [S02 p.18; S01 p.10; S02 p.6]

4. **Simplification outlook**: If a method/model works, derive the key wavelengths or spectral features that could be used in a simpler dedicated multi-wavelength or LED-based sensor; this is an outlook after successful screening, not an initial purchase recommendation. [S01 p.9]

**Comparison:** Compare MIR-ATR as primary with NIR and Raman on the same samples, using the independent laboratory water results as reference. Single-method models should be evaluated first; data fusion is considered only if no single method meets the objective.

**Go / no-go:** Before the test, agree numerical acceptance criteria with the client for bias, precision, range, response, and availability. A method proceeds only if its independent-sample error, bias and lag meet those criteria, and the chosen fiber link covers the needed range over the required distance.
