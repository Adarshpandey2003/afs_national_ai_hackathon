## Where each required item is in a report

| Required for each scenario | Section in `H0x.md` |
|---|---|
| Main process requirements and constraints | **Requirements**: stated facts, client premises (each with a verdict) and key unknowns |
| Clarification questions where important information is missing | **Clarification questions** (prioritised: P1 could change the method or setup) and **Assumptions** |
| Recommended spectroscopy method(s), spectral range, probe / measurement concept | **Recommendation** (methods to test, and what is not recommended), **Spectral range**, **Measurement concept** |
| Reasoning and supporting sources | The decision status and its rationale at the top; every claim is typed (fact, sourced, inference, assumption) and carries its citations, such as `S02 p.6` or `S01 Table 1 (p.6)`; the full list is under **Sources** |
| Risks, limitations and uncertainty | **Risks and uncertainty**, and the decision status |
| A short first feasibility-study plan | **Feasibility plan**: objective, samples, reference measurements, staged steps, what is compared, and the go / no-go basis |

Each report ends with **Verification**, the critic's checks: citations point to pages retrieved in that run, numbers with units appear in the sources or the client's text, all sections are present, every client premise has a verdict, and an expert review.

## Sources

The supplied AFS materials (S01–S06, the method comparison table and the reference document) are the only sources cited in H01–H06. The assistant can also cite published papers when the AFS materials have a gap. Such external citations always include the quoted passage, verified word for word against the paper's abstract (see D04 and D07). None of the six evaluation answers needed one.
